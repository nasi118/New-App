Trust architecture

Professional-grade AI should know what it knows — and what it does not.

The platform is being built around a simple standard: calculations should come from controlled engines, legal conclusions should be grounded in authority, and unresolved issues should be escalated rather than dressed up as certainty.

01 / CALCULATION

Engine-backed numbers

AI can explain and orchestrate. It does not get to invent the tax calculation simply because a report needs a number.

02 / AUTHORITY

Primary-source grounding

Material tax propositions should be supportable by approved authority rather than retrofitted with a citation after an answer is generated.

03 / JUDGMENT

Human AI

If the system cannot support a conclusion, it should stop, explain what remains unresolved, and route the issue for professional review.

Why this exists

Built from the reviewer's side of the table.

Sophisticated tax work is rarely difficult because someone cannot produce an answer. It is difficult because the answer has to survive facts, authority, calculation, review, and judgment.

AI Tax Strategy Advisors is an effort to build technology around that standard — not around the idea that a model should answer every question placed in front of it.

The goal is not “AI that knows taxes.” The goal is a planning system whose work can be inspected, challenged, and reviewed.
